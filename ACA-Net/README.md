# ACA-Net: supplementary material

Supplementary material of "Significance of Local and Global Context for Low-Complexity Speech
Enhancement": an interactive page (`index.html`) with three sections, in this order.

**Audio examples.** Five VoiceBank+DEMAND test utterances (p232_246, p232_328, p232_267,
p232_052, p232_010), selected with the buttons 1 to 5. Each example shows the spectrograms of the
noisy signal, the outputs of the ACA-Net-M variants in Table V (a single-scale local context,
d = 1; multi-scale local contexts; the global context only; both the local and global contexts)
and the clean signal, in two rows of three, with an audio player under each spectrogram and the
PESQ of each output in its title. A paragraph of observations under the figure describes the
input noise and the differences between the variants.

**Context weights of ACA-Net.** For the test utterance p232_006 and a selected block
and the time or frequency ACA module of ACA-Net-M (VoiceBank+DEMAND model), the weights of the
five local contexts (dilations 1, 2, 4, 8, 16) and of the global context, on a 0 to 1 scale.
Above them, the noisy, clean and ACA-Net-M-enhanced spectrograms, each with an audio player.

**Attention maps of CMGAN and MP-SENet.** The attention maps of every head of every layer of
CMGAN and MP-SENet for the test utterance p232_006. A slider selects the frequency of the
time-attention maps (every 4th row of the model, 0.32 kHz apart, including the 2 kHz row of
Fig. 7) or the time of the frequency-attention maps (every 4th frame, 25 ms apart, including the
0.89 s frame of Fig. 7). Above the maps, four spectrograms, each with an audio player: the noisy
signal, the model's output with full attention, its output with every attention layer replaced by
the Local + Weighted pooling of Section V (`local_pool`, R = 16), and the clean signal. The two
enhanced signals are nearly indistinguishable, which is the point of the replacement analysis.

## Data

- `data/examples/<n>/`: for example n, `spec.png` (the six log spectrograms stacked vertically,
  70 dB range, magma palette) and the six signals as 16-bit WAV at 16 kHz (`noisy`, `single`,
  `multi`, `global`, `full`, `clean`); `data/examples/meta.js` lists the utterances, noise
  types, SNRs, PESQ values, observations and shapes. Generated by
  `scripts/audio_examples_webpage.py` of the ACA-Net code.
- `data/cmgan/`, `data/mpsenet/`: the attention maps as 8-bit PNGs with the magma palette
  (attention relative to uniform, log2 scale from 1/8 to 8); time maps are averaged over
  4 x 4 frames. `spec.png` is the noisy, full-attention, Local + Weighted and clean log
  spectrograms stacked vertically (70 dB range), and `noisy.wav`, `full.wav`, `lpw.wav`,
  `clean.wav` are those four signals as 16-bit WAV at 16 kHz.
- `data/acanet_m/`: the context weights of ACA-Net-M (0 to 1 mapped to the palette;
  one folder per ACA-Net model); `spec.png` (noisy, clean, enhanced) and `noisy.wav`,
  `clean.wav`, `enhanced.wav`.
- `data/meta.js`: the shapes of the attention maps and context weights. These data are
  generated by `scripts/attention_webpage.py` of the ACA-Net code; the audio and the
  spectrogram stacks of the two lower sections by `scripts/webpage_audio_attn.py`.

The page works both on GitHub Pages and when `index.html` is opened as a local file.
