# ACA-Net: attention maps of CMGAN and MP-SENet

Supplementary material of "Adaptive Context Aggregation With Linear Complexity for Dual-Path
Speech Enhancement": an interactive page (`index.html`) with the attention maps of every head
of every layer of CMGAN and MP-SENet for the VoiceBank+DEMAND test utterance p232_006. A slider
selects the frequency of the time-attention maps (every 4th row of the model, 0.32 kHz apart,
including the 2 kHz row of Fig. 7) or the time of the frequency-attention maps (every 4th
frame, 25 ms apart, including the 0.89 s frame of Fig. 7).

A second viewer shows the context-selection weights of ACA-Net-M (VoiceBank+DEMAND model) for
the same utterance: for a selected block and the time or frequency ACA module, the weights of
the five local contexts (dilations 1, 2, 4, 8, 16) and of the global context, on a 0 to 1 scale.

`data/` holds the maps as 8-bit PNGs with the magma palette (attention relative to uniform,
log2 scale from 1/8 to 8); `data/acanet_m/` holds the context-selection weights of ACA-Net-M
(0 to 1 mapped to the palette; one folder per ACA-Net model);
`data/meta.js` lists their shapes. Time maps are averaged over
4 x 4 frames. The data are generated by `scripts/attention_webpage.py` of the ACA-Net code.
The page works both on GitHub Pages and when `index.html` is opened as a local file.
